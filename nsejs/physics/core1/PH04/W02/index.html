1 · Predict before solving

Compute the stored elastic energy U_s = 0.5 * k * x^2 to find the kinetic energy at the moment of detachment.

2 · Attempt: protect your decisive work

A 2 kg toy cart starts from rest touching, but not attached to, a spring of stiffness 200 N m^-1 compressed by 0.10 m. The ground is smooth while the spring pushes and up to its natural length. Immediately after the spring reaches natural length, the cart enters a 0.50 m rough strip, where a constant 1 N friction force acts against travel. No motor or other horizontal force acts. Does the cart cross the strip, and what is its speed at the strip's far end?

Assistance hints (unlocked after first commit)

Compute the stored elastic energy U_s = 0.5 * k * x^2 to find the kinetic energy at the moment of detachment.

Recognize that the unattached spring exerts zero force once natural length is reached.

Apply W_net = Delta K solely across the rough strip using friction work W = -f * d.

3 · Model reconstruction and derivation key

1. Phase 1 (Spring contact): U_s = 0.5 * k * x^2 = 0.5 * 200 * 0.01 = 1.0 J ==> K_release = 1.0 J, v_release = 1.0 m/s. 2. Phase 2 (Rough strip entrance): Cart detaches at x = 0; spring does zero further work. 3. Work on rough strip: W_fric = -f * d = -1 * 0.50 = -0.50 J. 4. Work-energy theorem on strip: K_final = K_release + W_fric = 1.0 - 0.50 = 0.50 J. 5. Final velocity: v_final = sqrt(2 * 0.50 / 2) = sqrt(0.50) ≈ 0.707 m/s.

Canonical key: Stored spring energy: U_s = 0.5 * 200 * (0.10)^2 = 1.0 J. Speed at spring release: v_rel = sqrt(2 * 1.0 / 2) = 1.0 m s^-1. Friction work along rough strip: W_fric = -f * d = -1.0 * 0.50 = -0.50 J. Work to cross entire strip is 0.50 J, which is less than available 1.0 J kinetic energy, so the cart crosses the strip. Final kinetic energy: K_f = 1.0 - 0.50 = 0.50 J. Final speed: v_f = sqrt(2 * 0.50 / 2) = sqrt(0.50) ≈ 0.707 m s^-1.

4 · Self-diagnosis and misconception check

Did your initial reasoning encounter this common conceptual trap?

Misconception: An unattached spring continues to pull or accelerate the cart after passing its natural length.

Why it fails: A contact spring without physical attachment can only push under compression; it cannot exert tension to pull a detached mass.

5 · Targeted repair and conceptual correction

Repair procedure: Always divide the path into two distinct phases: contact phase (x > 0) and uncoupled phase (x <= 0).

6 · Boundary condition transfer test

Changed boundary prompt: What is the minimum length of the rough strip required to bring the cart to a complete stop?

Boundary model solution: For v_final = 0, W_fric = -K_release ==> -1 * d_stop = -1.0 J ==> d_stop = 1.0 m.
