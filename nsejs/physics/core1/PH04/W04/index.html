1 · Predict before solving

Compute the linear mass density lambda = M / L in kg/m.

2 · Attempt: protect your decisive work

An 8 m uniform rope has mass 4 kg. Initially 4 m of the rope hang vertically below a smooth platform edge. A student slowly pulls the rope upward until 2 m remain hanging. Neglect friction, rope stretch, and any change in kinetic energy. Use g = 10 m s^-2. How much work must the student do against gravity?

Assistance hints (unlocked after first commit)

Compute the linear mass density lambda = M / L in kg/m.

Calculate the potential energy for both states using U = -0.5 * lambda * g * x^2.

Subtract the initial potential energy from the final potential energy: W = U_2 - U_1.

3 · Model reconstruction and derivation key

1. Determine linear density: lambda = M / L = 4 / 8 = 0.5 kg/m. 2. State 1: 4 m hanging ==> m_1 = 2 kg, c.o.m depth = 2 m ==> U_1 = -(2)(10)(2) = -40 J. 3. State 2: 2 m hanging ==> m_2 = 1 kg, c.o.m depth = 1 m ==> U_2 = -(1)(10)(1) = -10 J. 4. Work against gravity: W = U_2 - U_1 = -10 - (-40) = +30 J. 5. Physical check: lifting the 2 m segment that moved onto the platform takes 30 J because it started at an average depth of 3 m below the platform (2 kg * 10 * 1.5 m effective rise = 30 J).

Canonical key: Linear mass density: lambda = 4 kg / 8 m = 0.5 kg m^-1. 1. Initial State (x_1 = 4 m hanging): Hanging mass m_1 = lambda * x_1 = 0.5 * 4 = 2 kg. Center of mass depth: h_1 = x_1 / 2 = 4 / 2 = 2 m. Initial potential energy: U_1 = -m_1 * g * h_1 = -(2)(10)(2) = -40 J. 2. Final State (x_2 = 2 m hanging): Hanging mass m_2 = lambda * x_2 = 0.5 * 2 = 1 kg. Center of mass depth: h_2 = x_2 / 2 = 2 / 2 = 1 m. Final potential energy: U_2 = -m_2 * g * h_2 = -(1)(10)(1) = -10 J. 3. Work Done Against Gravity: W_student = U_2 - U_1 = -10 - (-40) = +30 J.

4 · Self-diagnosis and misconception check

Did your initial reasoning encounter this common conceptual trap?

Misconception: To lift the hanging chain, multiply the total mass 2 kg by the hanging length 2 m to get work 40 J.

Why it fails: Only the hanging 1 kg portion changes height, and its center of mass rises by only 1 m, not the full 2 m.

5 · Targeted repair and conceptual correction

Repair procedure: Always use the hanging mass lambda*x and its center of mass depth x/2: U = -0.5*lambda*g*x^2.

6 · Boundary condition transfer test

Changed boundary prompt: From this intermediate 2 m hanging state, how much additional work is required to pull the remaining 2 m completely onto the platform?

Boundary model solution: Additional work W_rem = U_final - U_2 = 0 - (-10) = +10 J.
