2 · Attempt

A tone has period8 ms. A second tone has frequency 8/5 times the first. Their wavelengths differ by0.96 m in one uniform nondispersive medium. Recover both frequencies, speed, both wavelengths and predict wavelength of a 250 Hz tone. A reserved measurement is1.30 m exactly; test consistency.

Author-exposed reconstruction, diagnosis and repair

3 · Reconstruct

f₁=125 Hz, f₂=200 Hz; v=0.96/(1/125−1/200)=320 m/s. λ₁=2.56, λ₂=1.60 m. Third predicted1.28 m; exact1.30 fails common model.

Locate any inverted ratio, ms conversion or unjustified shared-speed assumption.

1 · Predict

Predict whether doubling frequency at common speed doubles or halves wavelength.

2 · Attempt

Two tones of frequencies150 Hz and twice that frequency have wavelength difference1.1 m. Infer common speed and both wavelengths. A third tone of period4 ms has observed wavelength1.32 m. Check all observations; state what is conditional if media differ.

Author-exposed reconstruction, diagnosis and repair

3 · Reconstruct

v=330 m/s; λ=2.2 and1.1 m. Third f=250 Hz, predicted1.32 m. Same-speed hypothesis consistent, not proof of identical conditions.

4 · Diagnose

Locate any inverted ratio, ms conversion or unjustified shared-speed assumption.

5 · Repair

Write the two v=fλ equations, subtract wavelength expressions and check each recovered product.

6 · Boundary test

If first two tones travel in different media, can difference and frequency ratio alone determine both speeds?

Boundary response criterion

No; one difference constrains two unknown speeds.
