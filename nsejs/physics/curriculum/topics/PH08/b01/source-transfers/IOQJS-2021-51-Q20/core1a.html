Scientific construction

A freely damped oscillator loses mechanical energy to its surroundings. Its amplitude envelope decreases while oscillation can continue. For a linear spring at displacement extrema, instantaneous kinetic energy is zero and stored mechanical energy is kA²/2; at other phases energy includes motion. Period is the time between corresponding extrema, not the spacing between opposite signs. Under a simple underdamped model with constant parameters, same-sign peaks can be equally spaced even as amplitudes decay. Different damping models can yield different frequencies; a sampled graph must be interpreted with its time and displacement axes. A fading detector signal alone need not establish source damping; propagation and sensor gain can also change.
