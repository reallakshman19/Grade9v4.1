Scientific construction

For a short pulse emitted at x=s between fixed parallel walls x=0 and x=L, receiver moves x_r=s+ut until contact. Unfold the reflecting interval to virtual sources at −s, 2L−s (first reflections) and s±2L (two reflections). In stationary medium speed v, each image arrival solves |x_r−x_image|=vt; keep only physically valid times before wall contact and correct propagation direction. For positive u<v, right-wall first arrival t=2(L−s)/(v+u); left-wall first arrival t=2s/(v−u). Two-reflection arrivals are 2L/(v±u). More distant images on each side cannot arrive earlier than a closer image on that side, but paths from different sides may interleave. Detection thresholds or pulse overlap can hide paths. The original 2018 JS511 Q16 was deleted: under normal motion its first three times are not offered. This extension is original diagnostic transfer, not scored exam evidence.
