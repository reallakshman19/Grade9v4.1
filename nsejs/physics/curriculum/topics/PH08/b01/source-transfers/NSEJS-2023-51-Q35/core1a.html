Scientific construction

For the same ideal gas composition and suitable fixed conditions, sound speed v=k√T, so v²/T is constant for absolute T. It is not proportional to the square root of a Celsius reading. A time measurement can include a fixed offset τ: t_obs=τ+L/v. At a matched thermal state, two different known path lengths give v from their time difference and τ by substitution. Repeating this at another temperature tests whether the inferred speed changes as predicted or whether an apparent change comes from detector timing. The same-gas model does not establish actual humidity, composition or flow. A dropped source question is useful as a warning about measurement/model assumptions, not as scored exam evidence.

Worked foundation

330 m/s, since 363/300=1.21. If a thermometer reports 90 °C rather than 363 K, use absolute temperature.

Worked reasoning construction

At 300 K, two paths 3 and 6 m yield 12 and22 ms. At 363 K, same paths yield 12 and 232/11 ms. Compare recovered speed and delay; check whether one common delay fits.

At 300 K v=300, τ=2 ms. At 363 K difference100/11 ms gives v330, τ=32/11 ms. Temperature relation fits speed, but common offset fails.
