Scientific construction

Near 4 °C pure water has maximum density at ordinary pressure. For fixed mass m its volume is m/ρ(T). A full vessel's internal capacity also changes with temperature. In a specified freely expanding isotropic solid, small-strain volume capacity is approximately V₀(1+3αΔT); α has unit K⁻¹. Compare hypothetical no-spill liquid volume with actual vessel capacity at the same temperature. If the liquid is larger, overflow may occur; if smaller, headspace remains. Overflow reduces mass and subsequent states require the new mass. Use independent trials that restart at the same initial state. The supplied density table and capacity model are idealized; no claim about every real vessel.
