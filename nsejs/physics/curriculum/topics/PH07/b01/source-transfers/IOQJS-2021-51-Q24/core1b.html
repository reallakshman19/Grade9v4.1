2 · Attempt

A room model maintains constant temperature in two 2-hour trials. Known equipment heat load is2 kW in both; occupancy heat is1 kW in A and4 kW in B. Other common heat leakage L is unknown. Electricity consumed by cooling is3 and5 kWh respectively. Assume one common COP and common L. Infer both from the two observations, predict electrical use in a reserved 3-hour trial with occupancy2.5 kW, and test an exact measured 6.5 kWh.

Electrical rates1.5 and2.5 kW. COP=(6−3)/(2.5−1.5)=3. L=4.5−3=1.5 kW. Reserved total6 kW, electric2 kW, energy6 kWh; exact6.5 fails model. No unique cause.

4 · Diagnose

Check kW versus kWh, leakage, accumulation and whether COP was incorrectly limited to 1.
