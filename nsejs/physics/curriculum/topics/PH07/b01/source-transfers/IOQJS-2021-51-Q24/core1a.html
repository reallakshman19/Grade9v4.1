Scientific construction

For a room at a maintained temperature, its internal energy is approximately constant, so removed heat equals net incoming heat under the stipulated model. People, equipment, sunlight and wall transfers can contribute. A cooling device's coefficient of performance COP_c=Q_removed/W_electric (or ratio of rates in steady conditions) can exceed one because the device transfers energy rather than converts all electrical work into cooling. For a complete cyclic device, heat rejected to surroundings equals heat removed plus electrical work if other terms are negligible. Do not call COP a conventional efficiency bounded by one. In a stylized exercise a supplied conversion ratio may be used as defined. Use kW for rate and kWh for energy. If temperature changes, include stored-energy change rather than applying a steady balance.

Worked foundation

A simulated room receives3 kW heat; cooler COP=2. Maintained for2 h. Find electrical energy and rejected heat.

Electric1.5 kW, 3 kWh; removed6 kWh; rejected9 kWh.

Worked construction

Matched room trial has load6 kW, cooler electrical2 kW and zero temperature trend. Another trial adds3 kW load but electrical remains2 kW with measured accumulation1 kW. Test common COP.

First COP3; second removal8 kW, COP4, so common COP3 fails under specified loads.

Limits and repair

Specified ideal or matched conditions only; supplied quantitative relations do not establish compulsory exam scope. Check kW versus kWh, leakage, accumulation and whether COP was incorrectly limited to 1. Write room and cooler balances separately, fit independent conditions, predict and check reserved trial.
