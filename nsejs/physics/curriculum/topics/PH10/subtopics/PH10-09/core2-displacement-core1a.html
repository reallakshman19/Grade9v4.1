Reasoning requirement

Recover both physical roots and relate their exchange to reciprocal image magnification while testing inverse data.

Build the scientific model

A fixed real object and screen are separated by D. A thin converging lens between them requires p+q=D and pq=FD. The equation p²−Dp+FD=0 has two distinct positive roots only if D>4F. Their separation L satisfies L²=D²−4FD, giving F=(D²−L²)/(4D). Interchange p and q to obtain magnifications m₁=−q/p, m₂=−p/q, so m₁m₂=+1. The ratio of larger to smaller absolute image height is ((D+L)/(D−L))², not merely the distance ratio.

Worked teaching anchor

For D=84 cm,L=42 cm: F=(7056−1764)/336=15.75 cm. Positions p=21,63 cm; |m|=3 and 1/3, so the height ratio is 9. At D=4F there is one position, not two.

Misconception and repair

Mistaking height ratio for one magnification, or accepting inverse data without checking the root constraints.

Find both conjugates from fixed sum/product, use absolute magnifications and their reciprocal invariant, and test D>4F.
