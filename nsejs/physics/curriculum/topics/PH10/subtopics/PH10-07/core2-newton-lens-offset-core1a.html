Newton focal offsets and physical conjugates

Reasoning goal

Combine focal-reference offsets with the real-conjugate constraint and independently check the resulting global image geometry.

Scientific construction

For real conjugates of a thin converging lens, p,q>F and pq=F(p+q). Let a=p−F and b=q−F. Then (p−F)(q−F)=F², hence ab=F². The signed lens equation is still needed to interpret coordinates when an offset is on the wrong side of a focus. Absolute offsets alone do not establish that a real screen image exists.

Worked anchor

a=5 cm,b=20 cm gives F=10 cm, p=15 cm,q=30 cm,m=−2. A real screen must be at +30 cm relative to the lens.

Common wrong route

Measuring all distances from the lens when the reported positions are relative to the foci, or accepting inconsistent bench data.

Convert offsets to p=F+a,q=F+b before computing separation/magnification; check signed conjugate equation.

Model boundary

If an object lies between the front focus and lens, why can the positive real-offset formula not establish a screen image?
