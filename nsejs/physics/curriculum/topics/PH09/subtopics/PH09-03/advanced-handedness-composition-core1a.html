Coordinate reflection and handedness

AUTHOR_EXPOSED · ACADEMIC HOLD

Original teaching and transfer; no qualified direct examination source for this construction. Independent QRT and source review HOLD.
Subtopic textbookFresh Core1B

Reasoning goal

Use an oriented non-collinear configuration to distinguish a single reflection from a rotation or even number of reflections, rather than rely on verbal left/right labels.

Scientific construction

A reflection in a plane flips only its normal component. In two dimensions its matrix has determinant −1, so the signed area/orientation of a non-collinear triangle reverses. Two reflections have determinant +1 and preserve handedness, but may rotate the orientation; two perpendicular reflection lines produce a 180° rotation. A single arrow cannot establish handedness, which requires an oriented basis or non-collinear figure.

Worked anchor

Reflect (1,1),(3,1),(1,2) across x=0. The signed double area changes from +2 to −2. Reflect again across y=0 and it returns to +2, although all positions change to their negatives.

Common wrong route

Equating any apparent left/right change with a single mirror reflection.

Track two independent displacement vectors and compute their determinant before naming the transformation.

Model boundary

Why does a collinear triple of markers fail to distinguish the handedness of a general planar isometry?
