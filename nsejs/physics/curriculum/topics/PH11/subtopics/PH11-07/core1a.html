Physical question and assumptions

Steady DC, ideal 12 V source, ideal wires, ideal ohmic resistors at unchanged temperature, no unintended crossing connections.

What you must already understand

  • Identifying common nodes
  • Ohm's law
  • Junction current conservation

Build the model, don't memorize the answer

Parallel elements share two nodes and thus potential difference. Current splits according to I=V/R; equivalent resistance follows 1/R_p=1/R_1+1/R_2.

Worked Core1A anchor (different from the later independent challenge)

A 6 Ω and a 3 Ω resistor are connected directly in parallel across an ideal 12 V supply. Compute the branch and total currents.

  1. The same 12 V is across each branch.
  2. I_6=12/6=2 A and I_3=12/3=4 A.
  3. Total current is 6 A; equivalent resistance is 12/6=2 Ω.

Independent physical consistency check: The parallel equivalent must be smaller than the smallest positive branch resistance; 2 Ω < 3 Ω.

Misconception and corrective question

Nearby components or crossing wires are automatically parallel; topology is determined by connected nodes, not picture proximity.

Repair prompt: Which physical condition and representation must be checked before applying the result? Explain why a result that happens to match one example may fail when an assumption changes.
