## Specific author-exposed science observations

- **L1 Q18**: Earlier SVG placed resistor boxes without reliably following the bridge arms. The repaired five-edge schematic now explicitly shows AC 2 Ω, CB 4 Ω, AD 3 Ω, DB 6 Ω and CD 5 Ω; KCL graph check yields 18/5 Ω. Independent figure reproduction is still required.
- **L1 Q24–Q25**: Bulb brightness and voltage division use *nominal rated resistance*. Hot-filament resistance changes in practice; this approximation and its limits must be communicated in Core1A, not turned into an unconditional fact.
- **L1 Q27**: The 10 A fuse answer is the worksheet's theoretical choice for a 2 kW / 220 V heater; installation wiring, inrush and protective coordination are safety-specific. Do not teach it as a household electrical installation instruction.
- **L2 Q05**: Bridge needs a defensible topology and nodal/Δ-Y witness; earlier H1/H2 prematurely disclosed the discriminating transformation. Hints were rewritten to orient without announcing the answer.
- **L2 Q10–Q11**: Node equipotential choices require source/sink symmetry plus KCL, not geometrical proximity.
- **L2 Q14–Q15**: Cube edge vs face-diagonal terminal placement changes the graph. Corrected the previous equal-split and potential-level errors. Cube reductions are **extension**, not silently promoted as required school syllabus.
- **L2 Q17–Q18**: Finite ladder boundary must not be conflated with infinite self-similarity; the candidate adapter records these as extension scope.
- **L2 Q20**: Passive-network monotonicity is a law with assumptions (positive resistances, fixed external terminals); no claim about arbitrary nonlinear/active networks.
