Free fall, apparent weight and weightlessness

A1 · Observe and ask

Astronauts floating inside the International Space Station (ISS) appear completely weightless. Yet at the station's altitude of 400 km, Earth's gravitational acceleration is roughly 8.7 m/s^2—nearly 90% of its value on the ground! The astronauts float not because gravity is absent, but because they and their spacecraft are falling together freely around the Earth.

Learning target

Formulate effective weight W_app = N = m(g +- a) in accelerated frames, proving that apparent weightlessness in orbit arises from common free-fall (N = 0), not absence of gravity.

A3 · Construct the model

Weight measured by a spring balance or scale is the normal reaction force N exerted by the support on the body. In a reference frame accelerating vertically with acceleration a: N - m*g = m*a ==> N = m*(g + a). If accelerating downward with a = g (free fall), N = m*(g - g) = 0. A scale reads zero because the scale accelerates downward at the exact same rate as the person, providing zero supporting contact force.

A4 · Derive and check the rule

Apparent weight: W_app = N = m * (g_eff) = m * (g - a_vertical_downward). In orbit, centripetal acceleration equals local gravitational acceleration: a_c = g(r) ==> normal force N = 0. Dimensions: [N] = M L T^-2. Validity: contact force measurement in accelerated non-inertial or inertial frames.

Check the frame, sign convention, unit and applicability before accepting a numerical result. For a new model the original scientific warrant still requires independent review.

A5 · Read the physical representation

A6 · Three progressive worked examples

FOUNDATION

Question: A 50 kg student stands on a weighing scale in an elevator accelerating upward at 2 m s^-2. Take g = 10 m s^-2.

Reasoned solution: N - mg = ma ==> N = m(g + a) = 50 * (10 + 2) = 50 * 12 = 600 N (reads 60 kg equivalent).

CONNECTED

Question: The same elevator now moves downward while slowing down at 3 m s^-2.

Reasoned solution: Slowing down while moving downward means upward acceleration: a = +3 m s^-2. N = m(g + a) = 50 * (10 + 3) = 650 N.

ADVANCED

Question: A pendulum of mass m and length L is suspended inside an elevator accelerating horizontally with acceleration a_x = g. What is its equilibrium angle and effective tension?

Reasoned solution: Effective gravity is g_eff = sqrt(g^2 + a_x^2) = sqrt(g^2 + g^2) = sqrt(2) * g. The pendulum hangs at angle tan(theta) = a_x / g = 1 ==> theta = 45° to the vertical, with tension T = m * g_eff = sqrt(2) * m * g.

A7 · Worked anchor and independent check

Problem: A spring balance in an elevator holds a 5 kg mass. What does the balance read when the elevator is: (1) ascending at constant speed 5 m/s, (2) descending with downward acceleration 4 m/s^2, (3) falling freely after its cables snap? Take g = 9.8 m/s^2.

Solution and check: (1) Constant velocity ==> a = 0 ==> T = m*g = 5 * 9.8 = 49.0 N. (2) Descending with downward acceleration a = 4 m/s^2 ==> T = m*(g - a) = 5 * (9.8 - 4.0) = 29.0 N. (3) Free fall ==> a = g ==> T = m*(g - g) = 0 N (apparent weightlessness).

A8 · Diagnose a tempting wrong idea

Tempting claim: There is zero gravity in space, which is why objects float in satellites.

Why it fails: Gravity extends infinitely throughout the universe. At satellite altitudes (300–400 km), gravity is almost 90% of surface gravity. Floating occurs because the satellite and occupants have the same centripetal acceleration a = g, meaning no contact force is needed between them.

Repair: Always separate true gravitational force F_g = G*M*m/r^2 (which is large in low Earth orbit) from contact support force N (which is zero in free fall).

A9 · Counterexample, limit and synthesis

Changed condition

Can apparent weight ever be negative?

A standard scale cannot pull downward (it cannot exert negative normal force). If downward acceleration exceeds g (a > g), the person hits the ceiling and normal force acts downward from the ceiling: N_ceiling = m*(a - g).

Independent exit check

What is the period of a simple pendulum inside an orbiting satellite?

Reveal the reasoned exit answer

The period formula is T = 2*pi*sqrt(L / g_eff). In orbit, the satellite is in free fall, so g_eff = 0. Therefore T -> infinity. The pendulum does not oscillate at all; if displaced, it remains where placed.
