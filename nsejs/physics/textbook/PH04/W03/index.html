Constant power and non-linear velocity evolution

A1 · Observe and ask

An electric sports car delivering constant maximum engine power accelerates forcefully from a standstill, pushing passengers back into their seats. But as speed increases, the forward acceleration drops noticeably even though the engine continues outputting the same constant wattage.

Learning target

Deduce velocity and displacement scaling under constant mechanical power and distinguish from constant acceleration

A3 · Construct the model

Mechanical power represents the rate of doing work: P = dW/dt = dK/dt = F*v. When power P is constant, the body receives equal kinetic energy increments in equal time intervals: K(t) = K_0 + P*t. However, because kinetic energy depends quadratically on speed (K = 0.5*m*v^2), speed increases non-linearly: v(t) = sqrt(2*(K_0 + P*t)/m). Consequently, the driving force F = P/v and acceleration a = P/(m*v) decrease as speed rises. Acceleration is never constant under constant power.

A4 · Derive and check the rule

Constant power energy ledger: K(t) = K_0 + P * t = 0.5 * m * v(t)^2. Velocity evolution: v(t) = sqrt(2 * (K_0 + P * t) / m). For rest start (K_0 = 0): v(t) = sqrt(2 * P / m) * t^(1/2), and displacement s(t) = (2/3) * sqrt(2 * P / m) * t^(3/2). Force decay: F(t) = P / v(t) = m * a(t). Dimensions: [P] = M L^2 T^-3 (W). Validity: non-zero instantaneous velocity in an inertial frame, negligible resistive air drag.

Check the frame, sign convention, unit and applicability before accepting a numerical result. For a new model the original scientific warrant still requires independent review.

A5 · Read the physical representation

A6 · Three progressive worked examples

FOUNDATION

Question: A 2 kg sled at rest is powered by an 8 W motor for 4 s. Find its final kinetic energy and speed.

Reasoned solution: K = 8 * 4 = 32 J. v = sqrt(2 * 32 / 2) = sqrt(32) ≈ 5.66 m s^-1.

CONNECTED

Question: A 2 kg sled moving at 2 m s^-1 (K_0 = 4 J) receives 12 W for 4 s. Find its final kinetic energy and speed.

Reasoned solution: K(4) = 4 + 12*4 = 52 J ==> v = sqrt(2 * 52 / 2) = sqrt(52) ≈ 7.21 m s^-1.

ADVANCED

Question: Express displacement as a function of time for a 2 kg cart starting from rest under constant power P = 16 W.

Reasoned solution: v(t) = sqrt(2 * 16 / 2) * t^(1/2) = 4 * t^(1/2). Displacement s(t) = Integral_0^t 4 * t^(1/2) dt = (8/3) * t^(3/2). At t = 4 s: s = (8/3)*8 = 21.33 m.

A7 · Worked anchor and independent check

Problem: A 2 kg toy sled moves at 2 m/s on a level frictionless track. A motor delivers a steady mechanical power of 12 W for 4 seconds. How does its kinetic energy and speed change between t = 1 s and t = 4 s?

Solution and check: Initial kinetic energy: K_0 = 0.5 * 2 * (2)^2 = 4 J. At t = 1 s: K(1) = 4 + 12(1) = 16 J ==> v(1) = sqrt(2 * 16 / 2) = 4 m/s. At t = 4 s: K(4) = 4 + 12(4) = 52 J ==> v(4) = sqrt(2 * 52 / 2) = sqrt(52) ≈ 7.21 m/s. The kinetic energy increases linearly by 12 J/s, but the speed increases non-linearly.

A8 · Diagnose a tempting wrong idea

Tempting claim: Constant power means constant force and constant acceleration.

Why it fails: Power is P = F * v. If power is constant and speed v increases, the driving force F = P/v must decrease proportionally.

Repair: Always use P = dK/dt to solve for energy first: K(t) = K_0 + P*t, then deduce v = sqrt(2K/m).

A9 · Counterexample, limit and synthesis

Limiting case verification

As t -> infinity, acceleration a(t) -> 0 and velocity grows without bound in the absence of air resistance.

Unassisted synthesis challenge

Question: If a car of mass m starts from rest with constant power P, what is the ratio of its speed at t = 4 s to its speed at t = 1 s?

Model route: v(4) / v(1) = sqrt(4) / sqrt(1) = 2.0.
