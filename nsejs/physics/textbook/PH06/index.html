Build the physical picture before choosing an equation

1. Density describes material

Density is mass divided by volume, in kg/m³. A hollow object’s average density can differ from the density of its material. State which volume you used.

ρ = m / V

2. Pressure is not force

Pressure is normal force per area. At fixed pressure, doubling the area doubles the total pressure force on a flat surface. Shape matters if depth varies across the surface.

p = Fnormal / A

3. Hydrostatics needs a reference

In a uniform stationary liquid under gravity, gauge pressure increases by ρgh below the surface. Absolute pressure includes the pressure at that surface; moving fluid requires extra assumptions.

pabs = psurface + ρgh

4. Buoyancy needs displaced volume

The vertical pressure resultant on an immersed body equals the weight of the displaced fluid under the stated uniform-gravity model. Floating requires force balance, not automatic stable orientation.

Fb = ρfluidgVdisplaced

Three worked checkpoints

  1. Density: 600 g occupies 1000 cm³. Convert to 0.6 kg and 0.001 m³; density is 600 kg/m³. Do not substitute the container’s empty volume for the material volume.
  2. Pressure: At 2 m depth in water (ρ = 1000 kg/m³, g = 10 m/s²), gauge pressure is 20,000 Pa. A flat horizontal 0.02 m² plate at that uniform depth experiences 400 N of gauge-pressure force on one face relative to atmospheric pressure on its other face.
  3. Floating: A body of uniform average density 600 kg/m³ in water of density 1000 kg/m³ floats with 60% of its volume submerged, provided an equilibrium orientation exists and the body is not otherwise supported.

Independent quick check · not a graded Core1B attempt

At depth 3 m, a horizontal flat plate has area 0.015 m². It is in stationary water, ρ = 1000 kg/m³ and g = 10 m/s². The pressure on the other face is atmospheric. Calculate the net pressure force on the plate, in N.

Your answer stays private to this page; this is practice, not a mastery or source-exam score.

Reconstruct the reasoning

  1. Choose gauge pressure, because atmospheric pressure acts on the other face.
  2. Δp = 1000 × 10 × 3 = 30,000 Pa.
  3. ΔF = Δp × A = 30,000 × 0.015 = 450 N.
  4. Check units: Pa × m² = N. Using 3000 Pa or 0.15 m² would cause a factor-of-ten error.

Change one assumption: If the plate is vertical and spans several depths, pressure is not uniform across its surface; integrate over area or use mean depth only under the appropriate linear profile.
