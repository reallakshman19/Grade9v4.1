Genetics & Heredity — Epistasis & Meiosis Stoichiometry

Anchor Item: BIO-NSEJS-GEN-01 | Band: D2 | Cognitive Demand: MODEL | Crux Move: M2

National Standard Examination in Junior Science (NSEJS) Reference Item

Problem Statement

In sweet pea (Lathyrus odoratus), purple flower coloration requires functional enzymes produced by two independently assorting dominant alleles, C and P. Plants with genotypes C_pp, ccP_, or ccpp produce white flowers due to a biochemical pathway block.

Two dihybrid heterozygous purple-flowered plants (CcPp × CcPp) are crossed, yielding 1600 viable seeds. Determine the expected phenotypic ratio of Purple to White flowers in the progeny, and calculate the minimum total number of meiotic divisions required in the parent plants to produce the male and female gametes that formed these 1600 seeds.

Select Option:


Genetics & Cytogenetic Derivation & Proof

1. System Representation & Setup: Dihybrid cross CcPp × CcPp yields standard 16-cell Mendelian combinations: 9 C_P_, 3 C_pp, 3 ccP_, 1 ccpp. Functional purple anthocyanin requires dominant alleles at BOTH loci (C_P_).

2. Physical Model & Formulation: Genotypes C_pp, ccP_, and ccpp all lack one or both active enzymes, terminating the pathway prematurely at a colorless stage. Thus, phenotypic ratio = 9 Purple : (3 + 3 + 1) White = 9 : 7.

3. Execution & Resolution: Seed formation stoichiometry: Each seed requires 1 pollen grain and 1 embryo sac (egg cell). (a) Microsporogenesis: 1 microspore mother cell produces 4 functional pollen grains via meiosis ⇒ 1600 / 4 = 400 meiotic divisions. (b) Megasporogenesis: 1 megaspore mother cell produces 4 megaspores, of which 3 degenerate, leaving only 1 functional egg per meiosis ⇒ 1600 / 1 = 1600 meiotic divisions. (c) Total meiotic divisions = 400 + 1600 = 2000 divisions. Matching Option A.

4. Limiting Check: Total meiotic divisions for N angiosperm seeds is universally given by N/4 + N/1 = 5N/4. For N = 1600, 5 × 1600 / 4 = 2000.

Core 1A: Declarative Teaching Construction

Prerequisite

Biochemical Epistasis & Megaspore Degeneration

Complementary genes modify the dihybrid 9:3:3:1 ratio because enzymes function sequentially in a single pathway. In plant reproduction, female meiosis exhibits monosporic development where 3 megaspores degenerate, so 1 meiosis produces only 1 functional egg cell.

Worked Example

Duplicate Dominant Epistasis in Shepherd's Purse

Capsella bursa-pastoris capsule shape: AaBb × AaBb yields 800 seeds. Phenotypic ratio = 15 Triangular : 1 Ovoid. Meiotic divisions for 800 seeds: Male = 800 / 4 = 200; Female = 800 / 1 = 800. Total = 200 + 800 = 1000 meiotic divisions.

Independent Exit Check

400 Pea Seeds Meiosis Check

For 400 seeds: Male meiosis = 400 / 4 = 100; Female meiosis = 400 / 1 = 400. Total meiotic divisions = 100 + 400 = 500 divisions.

Stage 2 — Independent attempt (Fresh Transfer Twin: Duplicate Dominant Epistasis in Capsella)

Altered Epistatic Architecture: In shepherd's purse (Capsella bursa-pastoris), triangular seed capsule shape is governed by two duplicate dominant genes, A and B. A plant produces triangular capsules if it has at least one dominant allele at either locus (A_B_, A_bb, or aaB_). Only the double recessive genotype (aabb) produces ovoid capsules.

A dihybrid cross (AaBb × AaBb) is performed, yielding 800 viable seeds.

Tasks to solve: (A) Calculate the expected phenotypic ratio of Triangular to Ovoid capsules (15 : 1). (B) Calculate the expected number of ovoid seeds produced. (C) Calculate the minimum total number of meiotic divisions required in the parent plants to produce the male and female gametes that formed these 800 seeds.
