00 · Start here: the agent's mission

Build a learner's ability to perform the decisive intellectual work of an unfamiliar, authentic topic question. Core1A makes the reasoning intelligible and teachable. Core1B makes the learner perform it before it is revealed, then compare, diagnose, repair and transfer. A Core2 question remains its own source-bound assessment. The unit is successful when its evidence joins are defensible, not when a page looks polished or contains many questions.

Read first. This guide is an author-facing execution manual, not the master specification or a reviewer appointment. Read AGENTS.md, Start_here.md, CURRENT_CHECKPOINT.md, product scope, required reading, the architecture guidebook, and the Core2-led source pipeline. Resolve the actual owner instruction and master Issue #1. If the authoritative source is inaccessible, record HOLD and the precise retrieval failure; candidate-only work needs explicit scope permission. Do not silently substitute this guide for authority.

Platform method is reusable; content and academic rules are not. Physics, Mathematics and Chemistry have separate disciplinary warrants and Blueprints. The NSEJS preview also contains Biology: this guide can support an explicitly authorized Biology workpack but does not itself expand the three-subject product scope, approve a Biology Blueprint, or appoint its owner. Always identify the selected subject and examination.

DoDo not
Teach a transferable construction, derived from real source demands and scientific or disciplinary evidence.Write generic notes then attach unrelated hard practice and call it exam coverage.
Select the shortest valid route; protect the decisive move.Assign D3/D4 because the source is an Olympiad item, the explanation is long, or there are many calculations.
Create standalone Core1A/Core1B per authorized subtopic and integrate MAIN across genuinely connected subtopics.Force every operation cell to be filled or count duplicate links as unique questions.
Retain source fidelity, candidate status and exact version history.Invent source IDs, missing figures, keys, receipts, approval or learner mastery.

First handoff: issue a brief readback of scope, authority, pins, current baseline, workpack, exclusions and the next bounded deliverable. If asked for a blind review, do not read this author guide first; follow the source-only reviewer protocol.

Physics production update. The new section 00A is the required R2 evidence-gated entry after Owner adoption. The present status is PILOT, not approved production. Begin by comparing the teaching brief, Golden Sample SHA and evidence packet; do not self-certify academic qualification. The base guide still explains how to teach, and other subjects require their own approved quality contract.

00A · Physics R2: evidence-gated author submission and Golden Sample

Mandatory entry for Physics agents after adoption; pilot today. The existing chapters 01–27 explain how to create good Core1A/Core1B; R2 adds what an author must prove before asking for review. The current policy is PILOT_AWAITING_OWNER_GOLDEN, not an active publication/branch-protection gate. Read the R2 Quickstart and full R2 Quality Contract before starting any new Physics production workpack. R2 is Physics-specific: it does not silently change Mathematics, Chemistry or Biology subject rules.

Golden once, delegated batches thereafter

  1. Golden: Author one complete representative Core2 + real textbook Core1A + genuinely fresh Core1B. R00 checks code/bytes; independent R01 checks the science and demand, with real error and negative tests. The Owner accepts the versioned exemplar and standard once.
  2. Activation: Authorized integrator pins the accepted SHA and signed Owner-G0 receipt, changes policy from PILOT to ACTIVE and makes the workflow a required branch-protection check. Until this is actually configured, never say authors are technically blocked by production CI.
  3. Production: Every changed Physics content file and changed Core2 UID needs a grounded packet. Report sampled/unsampled independently.

Owner direction: Owner approval is required for the first accepted Golden Sample/standard, not separately for every routine question. R00 and R01 reviewers undertake the delegated continuing QA. The original cross-subject guide remains a teaching method, but the Physics R2 evidence contract controls claim promotion after explicit adoption.

01 · End-to-end execution map

Question route — what must be done?

Inventory source → exact question, variants, figures and key → SL1 → SL2 proposal under subject rules → independent QRT → native Core2 identity → required construction.

Exposition route — what must be taught?

Scope and syllabus → book, reference, experiment or proof → exact source units → claim/condition ledger → qualified disciplinary warrants → construction requirements.

Join on exact W, direction, conditions and source role ↓ Blueprint cell + topic adapter + warrant + construction binder ↓ Core1A teaching + fresh Core1B INSTANCE + cross-instance MAIN ↓ technical verification + independent B1–B7/academic decisions + owner release

The subject Blueprint, rubric and adapter are inputs to the question route, not things invented after selecting difficulty. Research and exposition preparation may proceed concurrently with source intake when authorized; the actual Owner's authoring sequence still governs. In the WPE pilot the specific gate is completed Core2 scope acceptance → Core1A acceptance → Core1B; never generalize an exception granted in a different workpack. A candidate may be useful before qualification, but the UI and records must say so.

03 · Research the syllabus and decompose the topic

Begin with an authorized topic and its curriculum/exam scope. Read the current official syllabus, prescribed text and edition, prerequisite statements and relevant reference material. Compare with the assessment corpus, including source questions whose keywords differ from the chapter title. A subtopic is a reusable construction, not a heading, exam year, band or isolated formula.

  1. Create a chapter concept map: entities, quantities/definitions, assumptions, laws or theorems, operations, representations, common errors, limiting cases and prerequisite relations.
  2. Split into stable subtopic IDs where the protected move or knowledge prerequisites materially differ. Combine fragments only when the learner must use them in the same construction; do not hide a cross-topic law inside a subtopic just to raise coverage.
  3. For each subtopic specify an observable competency: given X and permitted Y, independently construct/decide W by a valid route Z, state conditions, and check the result. Separate forward/reverse and direct/inverse constructions; directional asymmetry matters.
  4. Identify scope roles: syllabus-core candidate, prerequisite bridge, extension, outside owner scope. Mark any unsupplied syllabus or unselected content explicitly. A source's appearance in a chapter does not prove current syllabus eligibility.
  5. Build a prerequisite DAG (no cycles, no unresolved IDs). Classify each actual question at its decisive construction rather than stamping the entire subtopic D3/D4.
Example (illustrative only)Construction splitImportant boundary
Physics: motionSigned coordinate → distance/displacement → velocity → acceleration → graph translation → constant-a validity → relative motion → circular motion.Momentum/forces may use velocity or acceleration but are not thereby kinematics-primary.
Mathematics: linear constraintsRepresent condition as equation → solve within domain → test inconsistent/redundant constraints → prove parameter feasibility.Forward solution versus inverse parameter proof may be different operations.
Chemistry: stoichiometryBalanced equation → amount-of-substance mapping → limiting reagent → yield/mixture constraints → validate physical domain.Mass conservation is not equality of reactant mole counts; reaction conditions must be specified.
Biology (only if authorized): inheritanceAllele/genotype distinction → cross representation → probability under assumptions → test exception.A Mendelian model does not universally imply independence or complete dominance.
Deliverable. A scope matrix with stable IDs, prerequisites, official scope citations, direction, expected representations, competency and boundary. Do not select a new subject/topic or impose a universal number of subtopics.

04 · Search and source research protocol

Search is evidence discovery, not qualification. Use the highest-authority available sources and preserve the difference between what was located, visually checked, byte-verified and academically accepted. Work source-first: build the denominator before claiming coverage.

  1. Inventory the existing repository's Core2 registry, banks, source evidence, textbook references, previous topic maps, historical duplicate IDs and open gaps. Read exact metadata, not just a dashboard count. Source roles and source-year claims may disagree.
  2. Use official examination body archives: search exam name, year/session, set/code, question paper, final answer key, corrected key and syllabus. Follow the publisher archive rather than a stale deep link. Verify the printed paper header, page, language, code and actual question number. For a mathematical proof, use a trusted text or authoritative definition; for Chemistry/Biology, verify conditions and scope against suitable disciplinary references.
  3. Search both question routes and exposition routes. Search by equivalent descriptions, graph types, formula variants, alternate names, cross-topic contexts and original language if relevant. Include the full relevant corpus, not only easy-to-find exemplars. Read surrounding source passages, captions and diagram labels.
  4. For PDFs inspect the exact pages and figures visually, not only extracted text. Record exact printed page versus PDF index, source URL, retrieval date/status, edition, paper/code, question number, original stem/options, relevant assets and final key version. If originals are restricted keep them in authorized private custody. Hash actual bytes with SHA-256 where acquired; record unavailable hashes, never substitute a URL or fabricated digest.
  5. If a publisher PDF is unavailable, try the official archive and alternative official URLs. Secondary copies or indexed text may support provisional scope screening; label secondary/partial evidence and note discrepancies. Never treat a retyped diagram or coaching key as official custody. Keep the original and correction records separately.
  6. Independently solve before looking at the key where the review requires it. Reconcile final versus tentative keys, dropped questions, alternative answers, ambiguities and incorrect statements. Do not silently 'correct' an official key. Report contradiction or HOLD to the appropriate reviewer and preserve evidence.
  7. Deduplicate by canonical identity and exact content revision; distinguish multiple occurrences, versions, language variants and collisions. Do not equate similar topics or matching external ID strings. Cross-check bank UID/revision against registry UID/revision and source evidence.

06 · Smart Skills: identity before cognitive claims

Use native Smart Labelling 1/2 and the actual contracts. SL1 establishes source identity, integrity, paper/key/figure correspondence and practice eligibility; these are separate gates. SL2 proposes the intellectual requirement of an exact task; it does not own independent QRT or scientific review. Read native skill entry and integration contract. The owner-local R2 candidates may not be installed or accessible remotely. Verify actual skill, version and checker output before claiming they were used.

  1. Bind exact source occurrence, question revision, option order, figure revision, key version and source role. Run the appropriate SL1 integrity checker when available; preserve native report and its pins.
  2. Optional real learner P1 is evidence only when the original unassisted response and exposure history exist. Static question analysis can proceed with P1 absent/unknown. Do not invent learner evidence or block learning solely because P1 is absent.
  3. Write an independent solution as an author proposal before classifying. SL2 records the decisive work, shortest valid route, alternative routes and operations, conditions, factors, source role and evidence. Avoid retrofitting labels to pre-existing lessons.
  4. Pass exact records to the separately appointed QRT reviewer; preserve source-only first pass if requested. If a real skill is unavailable, return an explicit candidate research ledger, not a fabricated native SL report or success.
  5. Store native reports and reviewer receipts separately. A checker's exit zero is the selected mechanical gate only. No SL3, fabricated human review record or invented universal schema.

The authority chain is exact source → SL1 → proposed SL2 → independent QRT. The parallel source-exposition route still needs qualified warrants and the actual construction binder.

07 · Solve first: find the decisive construction W

Do not start writing a lesson from the final answer. For each exact source problem reconstruct the shortest valid route under its stated givens. Write what the learner must produce, what is already given, what must be inferred, and where an incorrect but plausible model diverges.

  1. Transcribe the requested result and all constraints exactly, including figure axes, option semantics, units, physical/mathematical domain and answer multiplicity. Check if the statement is underdetermined or contradictory.
  2. Solve with the simplest valid subject-appropriate law, definition, procedure or proof. Find a second shorter route, shortcut, symmetry, invariant, option-elimination route or counterexample where legitimate. A multiple-choice option can materially reduce work; score the real task including its options.
  3. Identify the one move that most directly decides success. Ask: if the learner were given this move, would the remaining work become routine? Conversely, if only the prerequisite ideas were given, what decision still remains? Record competing operations and why the selected one wins.
  4. Record the construction signature: ordered dependencies, direction of transformation, model/system and assumptions, representation invariant, decisive work, output/check and failure boundary. A signature is semantic, not a keyword match.
  5. Separate intrinsic difficulty from language, arithmetic clutter, many subparts, optional help, unfamiliar terminology or learner-specific difficulty. If the shortest route is simpler, change the nomination instead of inflating the lesson.
  6. Attach exact source role and scope. A source may be D2 while a separately authored transfer is D3/D4. Treat both as distinct scored tasks; do not rewrite original demand to match a desired cell.
QRT slotOperational interpretation (resolve exact template)
XThe precise crux, target, distinguishing condition, invariant or claim that the learner must confront.
YPrior demonstrated idea, relation, skill or anchor legitimately available to the learner; not the answer to this task.
ZThe route or bridge from Y toward X; support can orient without completing the decisive step.
WThe protected intellectual decision/inference/translation/bridge/warrant; what must remain learner-owned.
Example. A train-intercept source might be solved by a short positive-time tangency condition. An authored delayed-start/disjoint-window task additionally requires a constrained global minimum. They share a family but are not equal tasks. The original may be a short MODEL/APPLY candidate; the transfer may be a harder MODEL candidate after independent factor review.

08 · Seven operations: distinguish the decisive act

The seven labels form the current proposed common interface. Always consult the resolved Shared QRT template and applicable subject-specific column boundary; examples below explain the distinction, not an independent score. Do not label a task by the action word alone: a question that says 'explain' may demand a proof, and a long calculation may be APPLY.

OperationProtected W / evidenceCore1A constructionCore1B fresh move / common competing operation
RETRIEVESelect fact/category by defining condition; verdict plus discriminator.Rule, condition, near-neighbour, trap, self-check.Classify fresh cases with condition; if law choice decides, MODEL; execution, APPLY. D3.1 paused in current Physics.
EXPLAINCausal or mechanistic link from principle to outcome.Principle → each warranted link → changed-condition prediction.Fresh phenomenon; explain every link; proof of claim may be JUSTIFY, choice of law MODEL.

For every nomination ask which alternative operation would be most defensible on the shortest route, and what evidence falsifies the chosen label. Some exact tasks legitimately remain UNCERTAIN or MISMATCH until adjudicated. Do not split a single decisive question into seven artificial labels.

09 · Demand scoring and D3/D4 discipline

Score learner-owned work on the exact attempt or exam question, unassisted, against the authorized subject rubric. Shared Physics draft references the five 0–2 components below. Obtain the actual rubric anchors before deciding each integer; this table is a reading aid, not a replacement rubric for any subject.

Concept/model selectionIs the principle given, retrieved, discriminated or formulated?List genuinely plausible models and the assumption decision.

13 · Core1A: write a real textbook, not a task bank

Core1A should read as a coherent learning chapter. Start from a concrete phenomenon, example or disciplinary question, then develop conceptual understanding before procedures. A learner must be able to read each subtopic independently, reconstruct its reasoning and see how to check it. Do not make the learner chase unrelated links to understand a missing derivation.

Teaching sequenceEvidence that must be visible
1 · OrientPhenomenon/problem, why it matters, clear competency, conventions, knowns/unknowns; no exam answer leak.
2 · PrerequisitesShort bridge for each needed idea, diagnostic or exact prerequisite link; no assumed invisible knowledge.
3 · Build the ideaOrdered conceptual narrative: observation → model/definition → derivation → meaning → consequence. Each step has action and why valid.
4 · Conditions and limitsExplicit applicability, assumptions, units/signs/domain, exception and comparison with a tempting wrong model.

19 · Worked design mini-cases across subjects

These are original, illustrative training examples for authors. They do not assert source authenticity, selected subject scope, approved QRT labels or band scores. Use the actual subject Blueprint and review to qualify any real task. Each mini-case contrasts an anchor with a possible fresh transfer and a falsifier.

Physics · From measured acceleration to velocity and position

Anchor: teach a signed piecewise a–t graph starting from v₀, area increments v, then v–t area increments x. Work a single reversal and a zero-acceleration interval; independently check endpoint x and path. Transfer: new a–t graph with different nonzero initial velocity and two reversals; construct v–t and x–t, split absolute area at each zero, compare with changed initial state. W is preserving the derivative/area relationship through interval boundaries. A student who sets v=0 when a=0 needs the specific repair “zero increment preserves carried state.” A direct source problem with no reversal might be easier than the transfer. Candidate REPRESENT, not automatically D3.

Mathematics · Linear constraints and inverse feasibility

Anchor: teach elimination for two independent linear equations, with a check by substitution and the rank/consistency idea. Example x+y=8 and x−y=2 gives (5,3). Transfer: x+y=8 and 2x+2y=k, with x,y nonnegative integers; prove which k permits solutions, give the full feasible set, then add x−y=2 and decide uniqueness. The second equation is dependent, not a second independent constraint; k=16 is necessary and sufficient, and the added equation selects one feasible pair. W is feasibility/warrant and independence, not rote elimination. Boundary: if k changes, no solution even though each equation alone has solutions. This is a mathematical example, not a Physics cell calibration.

Chemistry · Limiting reagent, reaction conditions and inverse constraints

Anchor: teach mole ratios from a balanced reaction, limiting reagent by comparing reaction extents, excess and conservation; assume complete reaction under stated model. Original symbolic reaction 2A+B→C, with A and B amount measured in moles and no side reaction. Transfer: available A=6 mol, B=b mol, measured C=2 mol at completion under the ideal model; determine feasible b, identify limiting reagent and leftovers. C=min(3,b); b=2, A remaining2 mol, B exhausted. Boundary: if yield is only 50% of theoretical, observed 2 mol is impossible because max theoretical3 gives max1.5. W may be MODEL/JUSTIFY depending on the exact givens and route; check atom/charge balance for a real chemical equation rather than treating abstract A/B/C as a named chemistry reaction. Do not infer kinetics or equilibrium from ideal stoichiometry.

Biology · Conditional inference in inheritance (if authorized)

Anchor: under a single-locus complete-dominance model, teach genotype/phenotype, gamete probabilities and the Punnett representation. Transfer: a dominant-phenotype individual crossed with a known recessive homozygote produces a recessive offspring. Under the model and verified parentage the first parent must carry the recessive allele; infer genotype, then predict offspring probabilities and test changed penetrance assumptions. W is a conditional inference from observed outcome, not merely filling a table. One family sample does not guarantee future ratios; changed penetrance weakens the phenotype→genotype inference. A Biology-specific reviewer and approved rubric would be required.

Cross-subject lesson. Keep the transferable workflow and evidence discipline, not the Physics laws, factors' calibration or cell counts. Each subject's independent reviewer must evaluate actual assumptions and shortest routes. These mini-cases are author-exposed training material, never source-only review packets.

23 · Independent review, separation and release

Separate author, technical reviewer and academic/QRT reviewer. The Owner/Shared authority decides admission and release; neither author nor validator can manufacture external human receipts. Labels such as R00 technical and R01 independent academic are workpack-specific, not automatically appointed roles.

  1. Author hands off exact head, immutable artifact pins, source registers, candidate W/band and competing routes, anchor-versus-transfer novelty, B1–B7 evidence, source custody gaps, tests and explicit limitations. This is author-exposed material.
  2. Technical reviewer checks implementation, exact package and native contracts, complete browser journeys, leakage, state, source route and failure controls. Report REQUEST_CHANGES or scoped technical pass/HOLD with exact SHA and reproducible finding; not academic approval.
  3. For a cold first pass, dispatcher supplies only exact source/task/figure inputs, approved subject rubric, scope and seal destination. Withhold author solutions, keys, labels, review packet and prior exact-task outcomes. Reviewer independently solves, records conditions, shortest alternatives, W, five factors and ambiguity; seal bytes/digest before revealing prior material. If exposed, disclose, do not claim blindness.
  4. Separately reconcile source fidelity, scientific correctness, QRT/source role, teaching sufficiency, freshness, protected attempts and diagnosis/transfer. A source key conflict requires independent reconciliation, not silent alteration. Review exact new versions after changes; prior approval is not inherited.
  5. Owner verifies rights/privacy, trusted reviewer scope and digest, source eligibility, applicable gate outcomes, deployment destination and explicit release authorization. Missing or stale evidence is HOLD, contradiction FAIL. Do not merge/deploy protected content because a user asked for draft authoring or because tests passed.
Independent reviewer route. Use source-only entry for a sealed academic first pass. This page and any author solution packet are not suitable before sealing. Never auto-create an approval receipt, infer review authority from a name, or rename an author to create independence.
R2 authority correction: the Owner selects and approves one complete Golden exemplar/standard, not every new question. Delegated R00 technical and separate independent R01 Physics/QRT reviewers handle continuing risk-based batches using reviewer-owned forms. If the reviewer previously authored the task or saw its key, disclosure is mandatory and that review cannot be labelled blind. Return exact task SHA and specific defects.

24 · Batch strategy, coverage closure and corrections

Work in coherent slices sized for exact verification. Batch by shared construction, not by arbitrary question count: finish source-gap analysis, teaching, fresh tasks, MAIN membership, route, native tests and browser tests in the same revision. Avoid task inflation after a reviewer asks for corrections.

  1. Baseline: read previous green head and reviewer comments. Distinguish actual approved content from author candidate work; identify pending test and review gates.
  2. Select highest-impact source gaps: missing decisive construction, weak primary link, source role confusion, untested main journey or repeated anchor. Make a traceable acceptance table before implementing.
  3. Implement a bounded group of related construction gaps with exact source IDs and new task IDs. Preserve earlier working versions. If source identity isn't authorized, use locator-only proposals and keep counts separate.
  4. Revisit affected MAIN tasks and scoped routes; update teaching/adapter/support/ledger and native/browser regression together. Run exact-head tests, fix failures before expanding. Report any partial commit or unfinished batch explicitly.
  5. After engineering tests, hand off to independent review, not another expansion by default. If review identifies stale source, nonfresh assessment, wrong primary operation or missing test, correct that specific flaw in a new revision, reverify and disclose the changed scope.
  6. Complete the corpus audit even for excluded rows; report unscreened years, dropped items, secondary evidence, unresolved collisions, cross-topic owners and source gaps. Only claim closure at the level evidenced: author construction implemented vs independently qualified vs released.

PH01 lessons that generalize: a theorem copied into the protected task was mathematically correct but insufficiently fresh; MAIN selectors alone did not test complete journeys; a source URL resolved to a different paper; an external year was absent from the registry; and broad preparation links inflated apparent coverage. Corrective work introduced inverse designs, full journey tests, explicit paper locators and a separate association ledger. These are workflow lessons, not authority for a particular subject or current release.

R2 batch gate: freeze candidate UIDs and revisions before reviewer selection; require grounded packets for every changed item, an independently selected deterministic sample of at least 20% and one per topic, and 100% review for new Physics laws, D4 proofs, key disputes, failures and P0/P1 risk triggers. Unsampled items must not be described as individually reviewed. No routine Owner-per-question signatures.
