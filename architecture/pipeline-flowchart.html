Universal Governance Invariant (Rule 13 · AGENTS.md)

Green software test suites (passing unit tests, schema validators, headless viewports) certify only mechanical, layout, and syntactic compliance. They never grant academic release or student admission. All newly authored questions remain on HOLD: ACADEMIC_ADMISSION until authorized independent expert review certificates are signed.

Stage-by-Stage Reference, Templates & Benchmarks

Stage 1 · Question Intake, Source Custody & Fingerprinting

Stage 01

Segregate question items into Git-excluded custody (workspace/sources/<topic>/). Decompose each item into 3 independent files: stem text, diagram/raster, and official answer key. Calculate SHA-256 byte hashes and mint unique stable IDs.

Tools & Templates Used: templates/skills/smart-labelling-1/scripts/check_sl1.py templates/core2-exam-card/TEMPLATE-intake.json schemas/v1/source.schema.json

Measurable Pass BenchmarkExact 64-character SHA-256 cryptographic match between file bytes and metadata record. Zero duplicate IDs across the entire repository. Clear separation between EXAM_PAPER vs TEXTBOOK vs AUTHORED_TRANSFER.
Strict Falsifier / Failure TriggerModifying a single character in the question stem, swapping an image locator, or substituting a redrawn diagram without flagging as DERIVED_FIGURE immediately trips HASH_MISMATCH / INTEGRITY_FAIL.

Stage 2 · Cognitive Demand Profiling (SL1 ➔ Optional P1 ➔ SL2)

Stage 02

Trace the shortest valid solution route. Identify the decisive mental move. Score the 5 intrinsic complexity dimensions on a 0–2 scale. If real unassisted student attempt data exists, record P1; otherwise, explicitly set P1 to NOT_APPLICABLE (never fake student attempt history).

Tools & Templates Used: templates/skills/smart-labelling-2/scripts/check_sl2.py src/grade9qa/demand.py schemas/v1/assessment.schema.json

Measurable Pass BenchmarkEvery factor $c_i \in \{0, 1, 2\}$, total score $\sum c_i = \text{difficulty.score} \in [0, 10]$. Strict D-Band alignment: D1 (0–2), D2 (3–5), D3 (6–7), D4 (8–10). Zero empty factor evidence fields. Proposed status strictly marked PROPOSED / UNREVIEWED.
Strict Falsifier / Failure Trigger$\sum c_i \neq \text{score}$ or score outside declared D-band trips BAND_MISMATCH in demand.py. Generic justification text trips MISSING_DEMAND_EVIDENCE.

Stage 3 · Independent Blind QRT Adjudication

Stage 03

Assemble a sanitized, source-only packet (stripping author solutions, hints, and keys). An authorized independent expert solves the question blind, selects the primary cognitive operation, and assigns the coverage role.

Tools & Templates Used: grade9qa qrt-bind src/grade9qa/qrt_binding.py docs/agent-entry/07-independent-review.md

Measurable Pass BenchmarkPrimary operation must belong strictly to the 7 canonical verbs: RETRIEVE, EXPLAIN, APPLY, MODEL, REPRESENT, SYNTHESIZE, JUSTIFY. The first-pass review must be sealed with a SHA-256 digest before answer key exposure. Verified against owner-registry.json.
Strict Falsifier / Failure TriggerUse of non-canonical verbs (e.g., CALCULATE, SOLVE), self-review by the author, or unsealed evaluations immediately fails QRT admission.

Stage 4 · Disciplinary Warrants & Topic Adapter

Stage 04

Explicitly bound every formula and physical law. Document the exact physical claim, validity domain, assumptions, exceptions, and coordinate/sign conventions. Ensure directional consistency.

Tools & Templates Used: schemas/v1/warrant.schema.json templates/physics-blueprint/physics-blueprint.D3-D4.json grade9qa subjects-audit

Measurable Pass BenchmarkEvery warrant must contain non-empty arrays for assumptions, validity_domain, and exceptions. Taught reasoning direction must strictly match tested task direction ($A \to B \neq B \to A$).
Strict Falsifier / Failure TriggerReversing directional claims or omitting boundary assumptions (e.g., assuming $F = -GMm/r^2$ inside a hollow planet) trips WARRANT_DIRECTION_MISMATCH.

Stage 5 · Native Core2 Interactive Tablet Module & Learning Bridge

Stage 05

Construct the interactive, full-page student question card for 12.7-inch tablets. Structure explanations into 4 cognitive slots ($X$ Setup, $Y$ Law, $Z$ Formulation, $W$ Crux). Implement 3-tier progressive hints ($H_1, H_2, H_3$), commitment-first event handlers, and direct learning bridge routes into Stage 6 master lessons.

Tools & Templates Used: ag-core2 skill templates/core2-exam-card/TEMPLATE-bank.json nsejs/js/core2-learning-route.js architecture/production-checklist.html

Measurable Pass Benchmark0px horizontal scroll overflow across viewports (1280×800, 1024×768, 900×1280, 800×1280, 375×812). Zero iframes (document.querySelectorAll('iframe').length === 0). Touch targets $\ge 48\text{px}$ (WCAG AAA / 12.7" tablet standard). Derivations strictly inert (display: none) until commitment. SVG font size $\ge 14\text{px}$. Every candidate card or topic provides an active, verified route link to its Stage 6 Core 1A/1B master learning journey.
Strict Falsifier / Failure TriggerHorizontal scrollbar on any viewport, hint leaking Slot $W$, solution visible in DOM/alt text prior to commit, or isolated candidate questions with zero visible learning route bridge fails tablet audit.

Stage 6 · Core1A Master Lesson & Core1B Fresh Transfer

Stage 06

Author Core1A to sufficiently teach the decisive mental pivot. Author Core1B as an unseen twin problem testing the exact same reasoning signature under altered physical conditions. Tab 1 must render the complete Core 2 Reference Item with vector diagram and commitment box; never an empty disclaimer. Compile the B1–B7 witness ledger.

Tools & Templates Used: templates/skills/source-to-core1a-r2 templates/skills/source-to-core1a-r2/references/benchmarks.md docs/agent-entry/CURRENT_CONCEPT_COMPANION.md

Measurable Pass Benchmark (Non-Compensatory B1–B7 Rubric)All 7 witnesses satisfied: B1 Target (explicit demand & full Tab 1 question rendering), B2 Signature (distinctive pivot), B3 Sufficiency (Core1A teaches the crux), B4 Reconstruction (Core1B is fresh & unseen), B5 Protection (attempt secured before hints), B6 Diagnosis (diagnoses wrong models), B7 Provenance (expert physics sign-off). Tab 1 must display the complete target stem, vector SVG, and interactive options.
Strict Falsifier / Failure TriggerIf Core1B only alters numbers without testing altered physical boundaries, B4 fails. Missing first-attempt enforcement fails B5. Placeholder disclaimers in Tab 1 ("source authority note") without problem statement fails B1 Target. Non-compensatory rule: failing one witness holds the entire lesson.

Stage 7 · Dual-Engine Verification & Technical Preview Gate

Stage 07

Execute automated unit test suites, headless Chromium viewport regression audits, and build pipelines. Report mechanical compliance while strictly holding academic release.

Tools & Templates Used: pytest -q tools/preview_ui_regression.py .github/workflows/deploy.yaml

Measurable Pass Benchmark100% pass on pytest (schemas, DAG reference cycles, SHA hashes). 100% pass on Playwright tablet regression suite (0 console errors, 0px overflow). Status explicitly output as MECHANICAL_PASS / HOLD: ACADEMIC_ADMISSION. All questions strictly labeled {Unvalidated}.
Strict Falsifier / Failure TriggerAny automated test failure, broken link, or auto-generated human approval receipt aborts publication immediately. Software passing tests must never remove {Unvalidated} or claim academic authority.

Stage 8 · Independent Academic Release Authority & Admission Gate

Stage 08 (Final Gate)

The definitive non-compensatory gateway to student release. Independent subject matter experts (human Olympiad faculty) conduct scientific peer review, verify educational sufficiency, validate licensing/provenance, and issue cryptographically signed review receipts.

Tools & Templates Used: B1–B7 Review Ledger owner-registry.json schemas/v1/receipt.schema.json AGENTS.md Rules 6, 7, 8, 13

Measurable Pass Benchmark (Conditions to Remove {Unvalidated} & Rule 13 Banners)The {Unvalidated} and {Core 2: Unvalidated} badges and Rule 13 HOLD banners are removed ONLY when all 4 conditions are met:
  1. Human Scientific Sign-off: Independent subject matter expert certifies zero scientific contradictions, valid approximations, and accurate derivations.
  2. Signed B1–B7 Ledger: Complete human verification of target demand, teaching sufficiency, and fresh transfer.
  3. Source Rights Clearance: Legitimate examination copyright licensing or verified original authoring provenance.
  4. Cryptographic Receipt Promotion: Externally provided, scoped review receipt JSON placed in repo, switching status from HOLD to APPROVED.
Upon satisfaction, status transitions to {Academically Admitted}, and the warning banner converts to a signed, timestamped Academic Certification Dossier.
Strict Falsifier / Failure TriggerAI agents generating synthetic review receipts, removing {Unvalidated} solely based on passing CI/pytest, or releasing questions without signed human expert authority trips UNAUTHORIZED_ACADEMIC_PROMOTION / HARD_FAIL.
