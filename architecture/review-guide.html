For R00 technical reviewers and R01 independent Physics/QRT reviewers: audit the actual learner experience, test the science from first principles, challenge submitted evidence, and return a reproducible exact-revision verdict.

00 · What this guide does—and does not—govern

R00 · TechnicalR01 · Independent academicR2 policy currently PILOT

This page is the auditor-facing operating manual. The Core1A/Core1B Agent Field Guide explains how agents author curriculum-first textbook and protected learning journeys. The Core2-Led Source-to-Learning Pipeline explains the examination-question-first eight-stage route. Neither substitutes for this independent audit.

Curriculum-first evidence Audit complete scientific teaching, the subtopic scope and genuinely new Core1B tasks even when no authenticated exam question is available. Core2 linkage may remain explicitly unqualified.
Core2-led evidence Audit source question identity, stem/figure/key fidelity, cognitive profiling, the native question reader and justified connections to Core1A and Core1B.
Current activation fact: the repository's R2 policy remains PILOT_AWAITING_OWNER_GOLDEN and production_enabled=false. A green pilot workflow is not proof that authors are blocked by production branch protection. Do not claim the Golden Sample was accepted unless a real Owner receipt and active policy exist.

Scope boundary: this protocol evaluates education, science, source identity and runtime evidence. Redistribution-rights assessment is excluded from this quality audit at Owner direction. Source authenticity and figure completeness are still required.

01 · Authority, independence and reviewer eligibility

RolePermitted actionsProhibited claims/actions
AuthorConstruct teaching, solve problems, run self-checks, inject negative faults and submit an author evidence packet.Cannot sign R00/R01/Owner receipts or promote its own D-band, science, QRT or release claims.
R00 — Technical auditorVerify scope, exact files, hashes, CI/browser, actual learner state, coverage and contradictions; issue technical PASS-pending-academic, REQUEST_CHANGES or HOLD_BLOCKER.Cannot turn a green test into academic approval, forge R01 evidence or quietly rewrite the author's chapter under reviewer-only mode.
R01 — Independent Physics/QRT reviewerIndependently solve unseen tasks before reading the key, derive Physics, assess Core1A sufficiency, Core1B freshness, B1–B7 and exact cognitive demand.Cannot claim blind review if exposed to the solution, or approve an uninspected source/revision.
OwnerSelect and approve one representative Golden exemplar and standard, delegate routine batch review, authorize exceptional specification changes.Not required to approve each subsequent question.

Exposure declaration is mandatory. R00 previously contributed to PH01 and the C06 v1.1 interface. R00 may audit those technically but cannot independently issue a cold R01 academic verdict on them. A reviewer who has viewed an author's answer before the independent attempt must record EXPOSED.

In reviewer-only mode, corrections belong to the author/coding agent. The auditor returns an exact reproduction and required fix, then inspects the revised commit. If the Owner changes the auditor's role to fixer, disclose that role change and do not count the subsequent self-review as independent.

02 · Audit intake: freeze the real object under review

  1. Pin exact identity. Record PR and exact head/base SHA, branch, author workpack, topic/subtopic IDs, source item UIDs and the proposed denominator. Check merge status and parents; an unmerged PR is not production.
  2. Pin contracts. Record C01–C08 version, selected C06 v1/v1.1 rule, Shared 28-QRT source/blob, subject Physics Blueprint, adapter and any Golden Sample pin. Unselected or paused rules remain HOLD.
  3. Compare actual changed files. Enumerate full PR diff, deleted/renamed assets, other agents' paths, new figures, content packages, generated pages and tests. Challenge unexpected scope and silent rewriting of historical evidence.
  4. Verify author packet from bytes. Inspect the actual R2 packet schema and run the evidence validator. Confirm every cited file SHA-256, smallest relevant line span and verbatim excerpt. Reject recycled quotes or fake log labels.
  5. Compare declared with observed. The author may describe a passing suite, scientific law, learner behavior or source status. Independently check the underlying code/page, test trace, artifact receipt or source record before repeating that claim.
Automatic HOLD: wrong branch/contract, missing governing source, unresolvable citation, contradicted SHA, fabricated reviewer receipt, unowned path edit, or a new source claim with no authentication evidence. Continue unrelated review where safe, but do not certify the affected item.

03 · Physics science: five independently inspectable witnesses

The auditor must check physical truth, not just whether an equation exists. For R01, solve the task before opening the authored model solution; record an independent derivation and compare only afterwards.

WitnessRequired evidenceAuditor's falsifier
Primary law and derivationPhysical model, defined variables, assumptions, starting principle, valid steps and stated conclusion.Re-derive from first principles; locate the first unjustified inference.
Independent checkDifferent reasoning route, conservation check, graphical reconstruction or independent numeric substitution.Reproduce without copying the author's algebra.
Units and signsDimensions of both sides, coordinate convention, sign of physical quantity, conversions.Inject an omitted density/mass factor or reverse the axis; should expose a contradiction.
Model limitsValidity domain, regime assumptions and meaningful limiting cases.Apply at a boundary or beyond the valid interval and show the failure.
Counterexample/figureA counterexample to an attractive false generalization, physically accurate scientific diagram.Flip a force or ray, remove an axis or test impossible values; determine if the intended inference changes.

Example mandatory unit trap: for upward-positive height in a uniformly accelerating fluid container, dP/dy = −ρ(g+a), not −(g+a). The latter has incorrect dimensions; a lesson with the wrong scientific relation must not pass because its surrounding prose looks complete.

Scoring: each of law fidelity, causal teaching, representation, misconception diagnosis and independent checking is 0–3. A zero is HOLD, a one requires changes; a high average cannot compensate for a wrong equation. Mechanical hash compliance does not adjudicate Physics.

04 · Core1A: audit a real textbook, not answer scaffolding

Hide the Core2 source question, key and worked solution. Ask whether the chapter remains a coherent independent lesson and whether a new learner can reconstruct the required scientific idea from its own explanation.

A1–A3 · Intuition and mechanism Observable phenomenon, necessary prerequisites, conceptual cause before formula. No unexplained new variable or unsupported model jump.
A4–A5 · Warrant and visuals Derivation, assumptions, validity, accurate scientific axes/labels and a meaningful diagram that supports reasoning rather than decoration.
A6–A7 · Worked progression and misconception Foundation / Connected / Advanced examples with genuinely increasing reasoning; specific plausible wrong model, first error and repair.
A8–A9 · Boundary and synthesis Counterexample, limitations, an independently solvable concept check and an expected scientific explanation.

Auditor challenges

  • Remove the source question. Can the reader still explain why the law works, not merely use it?
  • Hide the worked anchor. Could the reader answer a new conceptual challenge using only the chapter's taught prerequisites?
  • Does the advanced example add a genuine physical inference or only more arithmetic and changed numbers?
  • Is every critical equation unit/sign checked and every graph/figure consistent with its scientific claim?
Reject B3 teaching sufficiency when the chapter consists only of formula boxes, abbreviated worked answers or stock hints; when the decisive physical move is not taught; or when diagrams are missing/misleading. Word count, headings and three examples alone are never proof of textbook quality.

05 · Core1B: prove genuinely fresh learner-owned work W

For each proposed INSTANCE and MAIN, inspect actual student-visible text—not only metadata. Create a side-by-side anchor-versus-transfer comparison and independently solve the shortest valid route.

CompareCore1A worked anchorCore1B fresh task
Given information and modelActual stated premises and assumptionsActual materially different premises/assumptions
Decisive work WModel choice, inference or proof already taughtWhich decision the learner must now independently make
Shortest valid solutionVerified full routeVerified full independent route and constraints
Competing wrong modelKnown shortcut and its failureNew boundary or misconception that makes blind copying fail
QRT operationTeaching aim, not automatically a QRT bandOne proposed primary operation and independently checked five-factor demand
Hard freshness rejection: remove numerical values from both problems. If the same model, algorithm or exact proof solves both without any new inference, mark B4 HOLD even if the wording or context changes. In addition, inspect the Core1A explanation for exact symbolic identities that give away the Core1B proof.

Six-stage protection and diagnosis

  1. Predict: learner commits a meaningful model/sign expectation, not an answer copied from a hint.
  2. Attempt: an independent first answer and content SHA are persisted before explanations or H1–H3 unlock. Storage failure is fail-closed.
  3. Reconstruct: full correct scientific solution is revealed only after successful saving; previous first answer immutable.
  4. Diagnose: identify a plausible first wrong inference, not a generic “check your calculation” message.
  5. Repair: learner writes revised reasoning separately without overwriting the first attempt.
  6. Boundary: learner first tackles a materially changed condition before seeing its model solution.

H1/H2/H3: clarify observation → correlate relevant relationships → open a route without disclosing decisive W. Check hints, pre-rendered DOM, SVG labels and source-return links for early solution leakage.

C06 v1.1 integrated MAIN: one MAIN per populated QRT cell with at least two distinct subtopic instances, all bound members and a genuine joint inference. Metadata listing several instances is not proof that the task uses them together.

06 · Core2: examination fidelity, reasoning and honest reader status

Keep DIRECT_EXAM_EVIDENCE, PREREQUISITE_BRIDGE and AUTHORED_TRANSFER distinct. Audit original paper/year/set/question, exact source stem/options/figure/key digest when an original is claimed. A candidate or preparation association is not an authenticated exam question.

  • Complete visual question: if a required graph, force diagram, ray or circuit figure is missing, the affected question remains HOLD. Do not silently delete a [FIG] marker and proceed as if complete.
  • Independent answer: derive the correct result with units, constraints and explanation. A key letter without a physically coherent route is not sufficient.
  • Commitment-first UX: feedback and key remain locked until the first response is saved. Retry creates a new linked revision, never deletes the original attempt.
  • Canonical status: the displayed badge is derived from genuine qualified source/academic state; hardcoded {Reviewed} or peer-audit claims are prohibited without receipts.
  • Learning join: verify exact question UID → scientific demand → Core1A teaching → fresh Core1B task. If qualification is missing, show a truthful gap instead of invented completeness.
The question-first Core2 production stages are documented in the separately renamed Core2-Led Source-to-Learning Pipeline. A curriculum-first Core1A chapter may proceed without authentic Core2 exam coverage; the missing exam-source link must remain explicit.

07 · QRT and B1–B7: verify the mental move independently

For a claimed QRT task, determine one primary operation from the actual shortest reasoning route, not the author's title. The seven Shared operations are RETRIEVE, EXPLAIN, APPLY, MODEL, REPRESENT, SYNTHESIZE and JUSTIFY. Apply the source-pinned five factors (each 0–2), and disclose disputed or paused Blueprint cells. A long calculation is not automatically D4.

WitnessMinimum auditor finding
B1 TargetAppropriate scientific learning target, prerequisites and scoped source.
B2 ConstructionCorrect primary operation, protected W and five-factor demand evidence.
B3 Core1AActual teaching of the decisive inference, checked cold-read and full concept explanation.
B4 Core1BUnseen transfer requiring a new genuine inference, not a numerical clone.
B5 ProtectionImmutable first response, revision-specific digest, pre-first help state and no leak.
B6 Repair/BoundarySpecific first-error diagnosis, learner repair and changed-condition reasoning.
B7 Physics/ProvenanceDefensible laws, sign/limits, diagram, source-role fidelity and exact revision.

R00 may validate task bindings, score arithmetic and evidence integrity. Only an eligible, genuinely unexposed R01 can present an independent scientific/QRT/B1–B7 academic determination. Every witness is noncompensatory: another PASS cannot substitute for a HOLD.

08 · Exact-head engineering, HTTP Chromium and fault injection

Run or independently inspect unchanged full Python QA and real served HTTP Chromium. Confirm the **same exact content head** as the audited PR, and record job IDs, artifacts and untested paths. A successful synthetic fixture is not a student-page end-to-end receipt.

12.7-inch tablet 1000×1600 portrait and 1600×1000 landscape; keyboard/touch, no horizontal overflow, navigation, refresh, readable figures.
Protected evidence First answer/choice before hint or solution; failed storage, reset, stale content, source/textbook return, H1–H3 exposure and boundary gating.

09 · Question-level coverage and cross-agent reconciliation

Audit a stable UID + exact revision per task. Reconcile every source question to its exact Core1A teaching and Core1B INSTANCE/MAIN coverage. Record source roles, missing dependencies, duplicate IDs, historical aliases and the selected contract version. Never substitute 56 authored cycles for 56 independently qualified exam questions.

10 · Decisions, P0/P1/P2 and noncompensatory STOP rules

SeverityExamplesRequired action
P0 — HOLD_BLOCKERMaterially wrong Physics, forged evidence, false source/approval label, leaked protected W, failed first save bypass, harmful merged ownership, silent schema downgrade.Hold affected task/batch, independently inspect same defect family, return exact reproduction and protect against release.
P1 — REQUEST_CHANGESCore1A too thin, Core1B clone, incorrect or incomplete example, inadequate diagnosis, missing evidence, broken keyboard state, source/coverage overclaim.Agent fixes actual content and regenerates packet/tests; reviewer reevaluates exact revision.
P2 — DOCUMENT_FOLLOWUPNonblocking wording, figure readability, extra explanatory example, presentation improvement without misrepresentation.Record with owner and due condition; promote if it affects teaching or safety of conclusions.

R00 dispositions: HOLD_BLOCKER, REQUEST_CHANGES, TECHNICAL_PASS_PENDING_ACADEMIC. An independent R01 receipt has its own scoped decision and cannot be created by the author or R00 impersonation. A reviewer should never suppress uncertainty to get a green badge.

Stop escalation: a P0 holds the affected batch and triggers full same-family inspection; repeated P1 failures require remediation and a fresh bounded demonstration before continued production. All test failures/warnings and new D4 proofs receive targeted review. “All tests pass” cannot override one incorrect scientific law.

12 · One-time Golden Sample, then delegated risk-based batches

  1. Select one representative exemplar. Complete Core2 + substantial scientific Core1A + genuinely fresh protected Core1B, with all eight negative-test families and full source/figure/science witnesses.
  2. Audit it independently. R00 tests bytes/code/browser; a separate eligible R01 cold-solves and examines cognitive, science and B1–B7. The Owner then accepts the exact Golden standard once, not question by question.
  3. Activate policy explicitly. Pin Golden SHA and Owner receipt; configure real GitHub required status checks, protected reviewer-owned receipt paths and CODEOWNERS. The policy file alone does not enforce permissions.
  4. Delegate continuing batches. Require an evidence packet for every new/changed item; independently review a reproducible reviewer-selected sample of at least 20% and one per topic per batch (proposed policy), with 100% targeted inspection for new laws, D4 proofs, key disputes, source contradictions and defects.
  5. Escalate by evidence. A P0 holds the affected batch; review 100% of the defect family. Report individually reviewed versus accepted-by-delegated-policy versus not-reviewed counts separately.
No repeated Owner approval for routine questions. Owner intervention is reserved for the first accepted Golden, material changes to the standard, and exceptional disputes requiring Owner authority. This risk-sampling policy is a proposed production contract until actually activated.
