Blind Review Protocol: Fresh independent reviewers must receive a sealed, source-only packet (07-independent-review.md) with hints, classifications, and answer keys withheld until the first pass is cryptographically sealed. 02 · The Two Evidence Routes & The Join

Curriculum authoring proceeds along two parallel evidence paths that meet at the construction requirement:

  • Route A (Question Route): Exact source task → SL1 custody → optional P1 (real attempt only) → SL2 proposal → independent QRT adjudication → native Core2 card.
  • Route B (Exposition Route): Syllabus/textbook units → condition/claim register → qualified disciplinary warrants → construction obligations → Core1A master lesson.

The Join Rule: Keyword matching (e.g. "gravity" or "energy") does not establish a join. The reviewer must verify the explicit connection between the exact task's decisive transformation and the specific warranted law and conditions.

Stage 01 · Question Intake, Source Custody & Fingerprinting

Verify that source stems, figures, and answer keys are segregated into Git-excluded custody with immutable fingerprints.

✓SHA-256 Byte Parity: Exact 64-character hash match between disk bytes and metadata records.
✓Unique Stable IDs: Zero duplicate question IDs across the entire repository.
✓Custody Role Tagging: Item clearly designated as EXAM_PAPER, TEXTBOOK, or AUTHORED_TRANSFER.
✓Diagram Custody: Original figures intact; redrawn diagrams tagged as DERIVED_FIGURE.
Strict FalsifierSingle-character stem modification or swapped diagram locator trips INTEGRITY_FAIL.

Stage 02 · Cognitive Demand Profiling (SL1 ➔ SL2)

Derive demand from the shortest valid solution route, scored across 5 intrinsic factors in $[0..2]$.

✓Shortest Route: Evaluated on the shortest legitimate path, not verbose derivations.
✓5 Factors in [0..2]: concept_model_selection, representation_translation, reasoning_chain_length, algebra_computational_load, trap_exception_sensitivity.
✓Sum Monotonicity: $\sum c_i = \text{score} \in [0, 10]$ strictly matching D-bands (D1: 0–2, D2: 3–5, D3: 6–7, D4: 8–10).
✓P1 Honesty: Real student attempt data recorded or explicitly set to NOT_APPLICABLE.
✓Status Label: Explicitly marked PROPOSED / UNREVIEWED until independent QRT review.
Strict FalsifierFactor sum mismatch trips BAND_MISMATCH. Generic rationale trips MISSING_DEMAND_EVIDENCE.

Stage 03 · Independent Blind QRT Adjudication

Ensure independent determination of cognitive operations, demand vectors, and coverage roles.

✓7 Canonical Operations: Verb belongs strictly to RETRIEVE, EXPLAIN, APPLY, MODEL, REPRESENT, SYNTHESIZE, JUSTIFY.
✓Sealed First Pass: SHA-256 digest of first-pass review sealed before answer key exposure.
✓Coverage Role Discrimination: DIRECT_EXAM_EVIDENCE (fills exam quotas), PREREQUISITE_BRIDGE (support), or AUTHORED_TRANSFER (synthetic check).
Strict FalsifierNon-canonical verbs (e.g. CALCULATE, SOLVE) or unsealed first-pass evaluations trip immediate rejection.

Stage 04 · Disciplinary Warrants & Topic Adapter

Verify that physical laws and topic slices are bound with explicit validity limits and directional parity.

✓Non-Empty Warrant Bounds: Explicit assumptions, validity_domain, and exceptions.
✓Directional Parity ($A \to B \neq B \to A$): Taught direction matches tested direction.
✓Topic Adapter Only Rule: Builders edit only the Topic Adapter slice. Missing common slots require a formal Blueprint Gap Note, never ad-hoc Blueprint edits.
✓Subject Scoping: WPE is Physics-only; Mathematics and Chemistry require separate Blueprints and adapters.
Strict FalsifierReversing directional claims or omitting boundary limits trips WARRANT_DIRECTION_MISMATCH.

Stage 05 · Native Core2 Interactive Tablet Module

Audit 12.7-inch tablet ergonomics, commitment-first interaction, vector SVG diagram integrity, and progressive hint scaffolding.

✓0px Horizontal Overflow: Verified on $1280\times800$, $1024\times768$, $900\times1280$, $800\times1280$, and $375\times812$.
✓Zero Iframes: document.querySelectorAll('iframe').length === 0.
✓WCAG Touch Targets: Interactive buttons, radio labels, and options strictly $\ge 48\text{px}$ (WCAG AAA standard).
✓SVG Vector Typography: Vector text, axis labels, and annotations strictly $\ge 14\text{px}$ (`font-size="14"` or larger). No optical cramping.
✓Vector Diagram Zero Leakage: Inspect SVG `` elements in Tab 1. The diagram must depict only the physical setup (symbolic vector labels $\vec{F}_B, \vec{T}$, initial parameters) and ZERO post-derivation numerical values or solved ratios.
✓Commitment-First Derivations: Explanations inert (display: none) until answer commit.
✓Progressive Hints ($H_1, H_2, H_3$): Scaffolding never leaks Slot $W$ (the decisive crux).
Strict FalsifiersHorizontal scrollbar, hint leaking Slot $W$, SVG diagram displaying solved numbers ($T = 24\text{ N}, u_1 = 30$), or derivations accessible in DOM/alt text before commit fails tablet audit immediately.

Stage 06 · Core1A Master Lesson & Core1B Fresh Transfer (B1–B7)

Evaluate lesson modules against the non-compensatory 7-witness rubric.

Witness Verification Criterion Failure Trigger
B1 Target Explicit cognitive demand documented. Tab 1 must render the complete, self-contained reference stem, vector SVG, and interactive options; never an empty legal disclaimer. Vague target requirement or empty disclaimer without problem statement.
B2 Signature Distinctive mental pivot and prerequisite model identified with one of the 7 canonical verbs. Routine formula plug-in without pivot or non-canonical verb.
B3 Sufficiency Core1A fully teaches the decisive crux without leaking the Core 2 numerical answer in exposition or vector diagrams. Explanations skip non-trivial steps or SVG diagram prints solved values.
B4 Reconstruction Core1B is a fresh, unseen twin testing the same invariant under altered physical boundary conditions (e.g. asymmetric masses, differing acceleration frame). Anti-Isomorphism Falsifier: Verbatim repetition of Core 2 problem or cosmetic number swap without altered boundaries.
B5 Protection Real unassisted first attempt secured and frozen ($\ge 20$ chars or radio selection) before hints or solutions unlock. Hints or worked derivations visible or accessible prior to commitment.
B6 Diagnosis Structured diagnostic catalog classifying common student failure modes (traps) with concrete, falsifiable repair steps. Generic "Incorrect" notice without diagnostic mental model mapping.
B7 Provenance Verified disciplinary warrant, bounded validity domain, and immutable SHA-256 asset pins. Missing reviewer hash, unpinned assets, or unbounded formulas.
Non-Compensatory RuleFailing any single witness holds the entire lesson module. High scores in other witnesses cannot compensate.

Stage 07 · Dual-Engine Verification & Technical Preview Gate

Execute automated unit tests and Playwright headless browser viewport audits.

✓100% Pytest Pass: Unit tests, schema checks, and DAG cycle audits pass completely.
✓100% Playwright Pass: Tablet regression audits pass across all 5 standard viewports (0px overflow, 0 console errors).
✓Fail-Closed Gate Retained: Status explicitly output as HOLD: ACADEMIC_ADMISSION. All questions strictly labeled {Unvalidated}.
Strict FalsifierTest failures, console errors, or auto-generated human approval receipts abort publication immediately. Green CI never implies academic approval.

Stage 08 · Independent Academic Release Authority & Admission Gate

The definitive non-compensatory gateway to student release. Independent subject matter experts (human Olympiad faculty) conduct scientific peer review, verify educational sufficiency, validate licensing/provenance, and issue cryptographically signed review receipts.

The 4 Mandatory Conditions to Remove {Unvalidated} & Rule 13 Banners The {Unvalidated} and {Core 2: Unvalidated} badges and Rule 13 HOLD banners are removed ONLY when all 4 conditions are satisfied:
  1. Human Scientific Sign-off: Independent subject matter expert certifies zero scientific contradictions, valid approximations, and accurate derivations.
  2. Signed B1–B7 Review Ledger: Complete human verification of target demand, teaching sufficiency, and fresh transfer twin validity.
  3. Source Rights Clearance: Legitimate examination copyright licensing or verified original authoring provenance.
  4. Cryptographic Receipt Promotion: Externally provided, scoped review receipt JSON placed in repository, switching status from HOLD to APPROVED.

Upon satisfaction, status transitions to {Academically Admitted}, and the warning banner converts to a signed, timestamped Academic Certification Dossier.

09 · Code vs. Authority Action Matrix

Reviewers and agents must strictly separate mechanical repairs from authority-bound gates:

Subsystem What Can Be Fixed Mechanically What Requires External Authority (HOLD)
Sources Path bindings, file formatting, JSON schema alignment Official examination authenticity, answer-key provenance
Demand & SL2 Score arithmetic ($\sum c_i$), evidence field formatting Real student attempt history (P1), D-band qualification
QRT Adjudication Schema conformance, canonical verb validation Decisive operation selection, direct-vs-bridge role
Warrants & Blueprint Schema syntax, cell mapping, hash pins Scientific law correctness, Blueprint approval
Core2 Tablet UX Viewport overflows, touch targets ($\ge 48\text{px}$), SVG typography ($\ge 14\text{px}$), inert derivations Academic admission to curriculum reader
Core1A / Core1B Layout, DOM structure, purging SVG answer leaks, button states B3 teaching sufficiency, B4 fresh twin validity
Release Gate Test harnesses, linting, Playwright regression Removing {Unvalidated} label, student release authorization
